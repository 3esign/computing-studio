THE SEMESTER THEME / DIGITAL PASSPORT

Give a building
a memory.

In a group of two or three, choose one object and one research track. Follow its hidden information and begin a digital passport: a record that connects claims, sources and the object they describe.

Trust is built into the record: each claim can carry its source, its history and its uncertainty. This is the blockchain idea behind trustless building—not trust without people, but trust that does not rest on a single person’s word.

WHERE WE ARE NOW

The passport is not a form yet.

There is no standardised digital passport to copy. The structure of the record will follow from the questions the groups actually need to answer—so the course keeps the frame and lets the content be researched.

    YOUR PASSPORT / 0 / 16

    Start with one claim you can prove.

    A source is part of the data. A missing source is also data. A story or interpretation belongs in the passport when its origin and status are visible.

    THE PASSPORT IS A NETWORK

    A claim is never alone.

    Draw the object as a living information system: a claim connects to a place, a person, a document, a material and a question that remains open.

    REAL FILES / AVAILABLE NOW
