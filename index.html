Object 001

The current task

One object. A question worth answering.

The groups have chosen their objects and research tracks. We are now formulating questions, finding, checking and organising data. This pilot shows a possible record, not a standardised form or a completed building survey.

  1. Ask about your object

    Choose one object and a research track: stories, symbolism, authorship, construction, material origin or energy. Ask something specific.

  2. Find and check the evidence

    Look for original documents, maps, measurements and attributed accounts. Record the author, date and exact source. Check whether different reports share the same origin.

  3. Connect it to the object

    Link each claim to what it describes and what supports it. Keep reported values, disagreements and unknowns distinct. A source is part of the data.
