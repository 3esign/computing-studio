What the film says

    Limits

    The record was extended on 7 October 2026. The MP4 preserves the 6 October version; this live player and the captions use the current record. A missing value is not proof that no record exists.

    Maps are orientation, not a survey base: rivers are straight lines through bridge coordinates, and the axis retains the earlier 430 m orientation. The 401.2 m scoping figure is a separate unresolved claim in the interactive passport.

    In the browser the film uses the fonts on your device, so line breaks can differ slightly from the MP4, which is the archived 6 October render.
