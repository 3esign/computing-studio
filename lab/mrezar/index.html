Mesh explorer · Mrežar

Compare a circular arc with straight chords. Change the number of segments and inspect the approximation error. CAD can also represent analytic curves; this instrument explores polygonal approximation.

What the numbers mean

The radius is 5 m. The slider counts segments around the full circle, while the drawing shows one quadrant. The error is R(1 − cos(π/n)): the maximum gap from one full chord to its arc.

The dome is a schematic wireframe, capped at 96 divisions around and 48 vertically for drawing. The triangle and STL figures are a nominal budget, 2n × round(n/2), with at least two vertical divisions; they are not a validated exported mesh. Pole degeneracies are included in that budget.

Try a change

Find the smallest full-circle segment count below 10 mm, then below 2 mm. Compare 24 and 48 segments: for sufficiently large n, doubling n approximately quarters this geometric error. This does not include manufacturing or measurement error.

The sliders and buttons work with a keyboard. Pause the rotation whenever it distracts from the comparison.
