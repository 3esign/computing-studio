Truss explorer · Rešetka

Move one of eight nodes in a thirteen-member truss. The model assembles a stiffness matrix and solves for forces and displacements. Drag a node, or select its number and enter coordinates below.

Model limits

A 2D pin-jointed, linear-elastic truss with small displacements, fixed axial stiffness EA and one vertical load. Self-weight, buckling, connections, failure and other load cases are omitted. This is not a capacity or code-compliance check. The 60 kN target is an exploration criterion, not a member capacity.

Read the forces

Red means compression, blue tension; line width represents magnitude. The triangle fixes both translations and the roller fixes the vertical translation. Select “Show member values” for signed forces: negative is compression, positive is tension.

Each member contributes EA/L to the stiffness equations. A very small pivot stops this solver. Near-collinear or collapsed geometry can be problematic; absence of a warning does not establish a well-conditioned or physically suitable model.

Try a change

Keep the load at 40 kN and explore whether every absolute axial force can stay at or below 60 kN. Then compare the result at 120 kN. Record a failed attempt as well as a successful one. This remains an idealised force calculation.

A nearly zero force in one load case is not sufficient reason to remove a member from a real structure.
