Route explorer · Panika izlaz

Edit a 48 × 26 grid with 0.5 m cells. Compare the geometric length of the selected route with the artificial cost used to choose it. Use the brush buttons on touchscreens, or edit a cell by coordinates.

This is not an evacuation assessment

The static graph omits crowds, travel time, evolving fire conditions and exit capacity. “Smoke” is an artificial cost penalty, not a physical model. The 35 m target is chosen for exploration, not taken from a regulation. A route does not establish safety.

Ways to edit

Choose fire, wall or erase, then drag or tap the grid. Drag the yellow point to move the start. Coordinate controls below can edit one exact cell or move the start to a free cell.

Straight and diagonal steps have different lengths. The artificial penalty changes route cost, not metres. Dijkstra searches this particular graph; a missing path is a result for that graph, not a complete safety finding.

Try a change

Block a corridor and compare route length with the straight-line distance. Then place an obstacle nearby and inspect whether route cost changes. Keep metres and cost units separate.

Compare layouts on paper as well. The graph gives one selected route; it does not simulate human decisions.
