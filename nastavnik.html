A room of 400

  1. Open a question and show its Q-code or link. Leave time to connect before the lesson.
  2. Ask for a private prediction, then a reason discussed in pairs. A silent thinker can use the written route.
  3. Sample reasons from different parts of the room. Reveal the explanation and try the changed case.
  4. Use projected text and prepared paper/accessible alternatives if Wi-Fi or a device fails.
